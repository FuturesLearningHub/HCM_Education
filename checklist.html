The HCM checklist

Every payroll cycle, before the cutoff

  • All timecards in your unit reviewed and approved, with real hours (overtime included).
  • Pending transactions submitted: hires, transfers, pay changes, assignment updates.
  • Any termination you know about is in Oracle with the correct date.
  • Leave actions routed through the proper process, never handled informally.
  • Backup approver engaged for any manager who cannot act before the deadline.

The day you learn about a change

  • Enter it in Oracle the same day, carrying its true effective date.
  • Retroactive change? Submit now and flag the real start date; never re-date it.
  • Resignation heard in passing still counts: enter it, then confirm the paperwork.
  • Upcoming leave mentioned? Point the employee into the leave process today.

Slow down and flag to PCB when…

  • A request touches overtime, hours, or hourly-versus-salaried status (FLSA).
  • Someone wants a custom title, level, or a pay rate outside the range (job architecture).
  • An employee will work from another state, especially New York, California, or Florida.
  • A termination involves a state with fast final-pay deadlines.
  • You are about to guess. Recognize and route; never diagnose alone.

Where to go, in order

  1. Oracle first: the job aid, guided journey, or HCM Academy course for the task.
  2. The HCM community: for judgment calls and how-do-you-handle-it questions.
  3. A help desk ticket: for genuine system problems and errors that will not save.

Never: the old email thread, the memory of a webinar, or a guess.

Every week

  • Run your standing sweep: unapproved timecards, pending transactions, anything aging.
  • Act on every reminder the first time it arrives; escalation is the avoidable step.
  • Check your Academy path for assigned courses and due dates.

The numbers to remember

  • $84,000 in documented overpayments across 24 cases in five months (Jan to May 2026, two units), mostly from delayed or incorrect actions.
  • 85%+ required training completion, within timeframes, tracked in Oracle.
  • 3 new states (NY, CA, FL), each with its own wage, hour, and leave rules.

The one habit

Same day, into Oracle. If you learned about it today, it is in Oracle today, with the true effective date. A sticky note is not a system of record, and payroll cannot pay from your inbox.
