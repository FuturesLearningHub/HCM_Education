My HCM commitment card

The paper version of the capstone: one task, one habit, one date. This card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · The task that slips

Which recurring HCM task slips most in your unit, and when does it bite?

2 · My habit

Circle one: same day, into Oracle · a standing weekly deadline sweep · Oracle first, before email or tickets · finish my assigned learning path. Why this one, for this task?

3 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: sit on changes until payroll week · open a ticket before checking Oracle · snooze reminders until they escalate.

4 · My feedback move

The most confusing process in my unit, and the fix it needs (training · guardrail · guided workflow · notification). Submit it through the HCM survey or community this week.

5 · First rep

Circle one: tomorrow · before the next payroll cutoff · within 7 days. Which task, specifically?

6 · The evidence line

Fill in after one full payroll cycle: what did not become a fire drill?

7 · Thirty days out

A month from now: is the task still slipping? What runs differently in your unit because of the habit?
